Scope

  per-IoU extraction and the plotting helpers with deterministic synthetic data.
  It does not evaluate the user's trained SSD, reproduce their reported pet
  detection scores, test CUDA/MPS execution, or verify checkpoint restoration.
  No real dataset or model weights were downloaded and no model was trained.
